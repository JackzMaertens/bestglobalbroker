Using an EA on a prop-firm account: what to check first

Prop firms (FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext and many more) let you trade the firm's capital if you follow their rules. An EA can help you respect them without emotion, but it can also lose you the account in an hour if it isn't built for them.

The rules that usually cost you the account

Each firm has its own and they change often, so always read your programme's terms. The most common ones are:

  • Maximum daily loss: often around 4–5% of the balance or equity at the start of the day. Floating losses count too.
  • Maximum total loss: often around 8–10% of the initial capital, fixed or trailing.
  • News: some firms ban opening or closing trades a few minutes before and after high-impact news, especially on funded accounts.
  • Weekends: some don't allow holding positions over the weekend.
  • Banned strategies: high-frequency trading, latency arbitrage, trading "against" another account or copying the same commercial EA across many accounts. Some ask you to get the EA approved first.
  • Consistency rules: no single day may make up too large a share of total profit.

What the EA must have

A stop loss on every trade

Without a stop, a single trade can exceed the daily loss. Rule out martingale, grid and averaging for prop accounts.

Its own daily loss limit, below the firm's

If the firm cuts you off at 5%, set the EA to stop earlier, for example at 2–3%. That leaves room for slippage and open trades.

Low risk per trade

At 0.3–0.5% per trade you need a very long losing streak to get near the daily limit. Check the maximum consecutive losses in the backtest and multiply by your risk.

News filter and Friday close

If your firm restricts news or weekends, the EA must be able to avoid them.

Add up the total risk if you run several EAs

If you run four EAs at 0.3% on the same account and they all lose on the same day, the losses add up. We explain it in how to run several EAs on one account.

Which JAIAK EAs fit

Trend Jury (2% daily limit), Breakout Rider (3% limit, news filter and Friday close) and Ice Cross (3% limit) have a daily loss limit, a consecutive-loss stop and a stop on every trade. Angle Shift has a stop on every trade but no built-in daily limit, so on a prop account use it with low risk. Always test on the firm's demo before the challenge.
