How it trades

  • Enters when the signal bar closes, in the direction of the PSAR flip.
  • Confirmation: ADX above 35 and rising with aligned DI lines, enough volatility (ATR) and Stochastic in favour.
  • Initial stop at 2.5 × ATR, capped at 800 points.
  • 50% partial close at 1.25 R (or when Stochastic reaches the opposite zone).
  • Break-even and PSAR trailing stop for the rest.
  • No new trade in the same direction until PSAR flips again.

Risk management

  • Lot size from risk % of balance (0.3% by default) and a margin check before every order.
  • 2% daily loss limit, stop after 3 consecutive losses and a 15-minute pause after a loss.
  • Spread filter and optional trading windows.

No martingale, no grid, no averaging: every trade has a stop loss from the moment it is opened.

FAQ

Why four indicators?

Because each one measures something different: direction (PSAR), strength (ADX), volatility (ATR) and momentum (Stochastic). Requiring them to agree removes many weak entries.

Does it work on prop-firm accounts?

It has a daily loss limit, a consecutive-loss stop and a stop loss on every trade, which fit typical prop-firm rules. Always check your firm's own rules.
