How it trades

  • Fast EMA 14 crosses slow EMA 38.

  • Break-even after 0.4 × ATR, 50% partial halfway to target and ATR trailing stop.

FAQ

Why is the win rate so high?

Many trades reach the protective break-even early; when price comes back they close at a small profit and count as wins. The profit factor (2.21) is the better measure.

Does it work on prop-firm accounts?

It has a daily loss limit, a consecutive-loss stop and a stop loss on every trade, which fit typical prop-firm rules. Check your firm's own rules.
