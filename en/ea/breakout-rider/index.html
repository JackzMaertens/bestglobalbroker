How it trades

  • Signal when the ATR(10) × 2.5 trailing line flips, confirmed on the next bar.
  • Enters only with at least 6 confirmation votes (ADX above 25).
  • Stop at 3 × ATR, target at 3 R.
  • Break-even at 0.8 R, 50% partial halfway to target and ATR trailing stop.
  • Closes an opposite position on a new signal and blocks re-entry in the same direction after a stop.

Risk management

  • Lot size from risk % of balance (0.3% by default) and a free-margin check.
  • 3% daily loss limit, stop after 3 consecutive losses, 15-minute cooldown and Friday close at 21:00 server time.
  • Optional news filter using the MetaTrader economic calendar.

No martingale, no grid, no averaging: every trade has a stop loss from the moment it is opened.

FAQ

Why so few trades?

The six-vote filter rejects most flips on purpose. Fewer, better-confirmed trades keep the drawdown low (1.6% in the test).

Does it work on prop-firm accounts?

It has a daily loss limit, a consecutive-loss stop, a Friday close and a stop loss on every trade, which fit typical prop-firm rules. Check your firm's own rules.
