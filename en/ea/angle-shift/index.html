How it trades

  • Measures the price slope over the last 24 bars and converts it to degrees with ATR(14).
  • Enters when the angle passes 15° in the trend direction (price vs EMA 110, efficiency ratio above 0.3).
  • Stop at 3 × ATR, target at 4/3 of the stop distance.
  • Closes 50% halfway to the target, locks break-even at 0.7 R and trails from 1.2 R.
  • 6-bar cooldown between trades, one position at a time.

Risk management

  • Lot size from risk % of balance (0.3% by default) or a fixed lot, with a margin check.
  • Stop loss on every trade from the moment it opens.
  • Order handling that respects the broker's freeze and stop levels.
  • No built-in daily loss limit: keep the risk per trade low.

No martingale, no grid, no averaging: every trade has a stop loss from the moment it is opened.

FAQ

Why M5?

The angle needs a few minutes of structure to be meaningful. On M5 it filters the M1 noise while still catching intraday momentum.

Does it work on prop-firm accounts?

Every trade has a fixed stop and risk is set as a % of balance, but Angle Shift has no built-in daily loss limit. Use low risk and check your firm's rules.
